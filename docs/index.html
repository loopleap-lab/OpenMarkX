MarkWeightX

A Toolkit for Watermarking Open-Weight Language Models

MarkWeightX trains a watermark into the weights of an open model, so text it writes can be traced back to it. It tests how much of the mark survives what users do to released weights: quantization and pruning leave most of it, merging and fine-tuning wear it down.

How it works

  1. EmbedThe owner trains one of 14 methods into the model (mwx.embed) and releases the weights. The key stays private.
  2. ModifyFine-tuning, merging, pruning, quantization and paraphrasing are built in (mwx.modify), so every method faces the same changes.
  3. DetectScore texts with the key (mwx.detect), or test a whole suspect model from its outputs (mwx.verify_model).
